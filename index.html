The problem

Running a business shouldn't mean guessing who owes what.

Most small businesses track money across three or four places at once — and none of them talk to each other.

  • A notebook for customer credit, until a page goes missing.
  • A chat thread you scroll through to remember who paid what.
  • A supplier bill you're sure you paid, but can't prove it.
  • No clear answer, at any moment, to "how much is the business actually owed?"
What's inside

Everything the shop needs, nothing it doesn't

Five modules that cover the whole flow of running a small business — from the first customer to the monthly numbers.

Customers

Save every customer once. Log a sale, mark what's been paid, and see who still owes you at a glance — no flipping back through old pages.

Suppliers

Track purchases and payments to the people who supply your stock, so a bill never quietly slips through the cracks.

Products

Keep a running list of what you sell, with pricing that stays accurate and consistent every time it's used on a sale.

Reports

Filter every transaction by day, week, or month, and see exactly where the money moved — and where it's still owed.

Dashboard

Total sales, amount received, and outstanding balances update the moment you record a transaction — no separate calculation required.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Expenso?

Expenso is a simple business manager for tracking customers, suppliers, products, sales and payments — built for shops, traders, and small businesses.

Is Expenso free to use?

Yes. Create an account and start tracking your customers, sales, and payments at no cost.

What currency does Expenso use?

Expenso displays amounts in Naira, so the figures match what your business actually deals in.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Expenso runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to install.

Can I see reports on my sales?

Yes. Filter your transactions by day, week, or month and see exactly how much came in, went out, and is still outstanding.
